About Xiangfeng Tea Group

From plantation to packaging, every step is carefully controlled

Home / About Us

Founded in 1998 and headquartered in Changsha, Xiangfeng Tea Group is a national key leading enterprise in agricultural industrialization. As a comprehensive industrial group with a full industry chain, full product categories, and full-domain marketing in the tea industry, our products span green tea, black tea, white tea, dark tea, flower and fruit tea, and other major categories. We sell across more than 20 provinces and major third-party e-commerce platforms in China, and export to over 60 countries and regions worldwide. Recognized as a Chinese Export Brand and a National High-Quality Development Base for Agricultural International Trade, we hold ISO series, HACCP, EU Organic, US-Canada Organic, Japan Organic, German Naturland, Rainforest Alliance, Fair Trade, FSSC 22000, and other international authoritative certifications — a promise of quality, traceability, and reliable global supply.

Production & Processing

From plantation to packaging, every step is carefully controlled

Harvesting

53,000 mu of organic tea plantation across 6 provinces. Hand-picked by 20,000+ skilled workers during peak season, ensuring only the finest buds and leaves are selected.

Sorting & Grading

Rigorous multi-stage selection process using both AI-powered optical sorters and experienced tea masters. Graded by leaf size, shape, color, and aroma profile.

Processing & Fermentation

State-of-the-art facilities with automated temperature and humidity control. Traditional craftsmanship combined with modern processing technology across 32 dedicated production lines.

Packaging & QC

Multi-layer quality inspection at every stage. Metal detection, microbiological testing, and sensory evaluation before final packaging in food-grade, export-compliant materials.

Global Shipping

Efficient logistics network reaching 60+ countries via sea freight, air cargo, and China-Europe Railway Express. Full customs documentation and certificate of origin provided.

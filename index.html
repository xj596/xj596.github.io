About AultrasTea

Brand Video

We are Guizhou Aultras Tea Co., Ltd., located in Anshun, the home of the Huangguoshu Waterfall. The company is jointly funded by Xiangfeng Tea Industry Group (a national key leading enterprise) and Anshun Waterfall Tea Industry (a state-owned leading agricultural enterprise). We specialize in EU-standard organic tea plantation management, bulk tea roughing and refining, and the production of green tea, white tea, black tea, and more. We own EU organic tea gardens and operate both national standard and EU organic workshops to prevent cross-contamination.

Our facilities include large-scale rough processing plants and efficient refining lines. We can customize products to meet the regulations of the EU, USA, Japan, Korea, and other countries. With strong support from our two shareholders in technology, supply chain, capital, and sales channels, we are a reliable source manufacturer integrating plantation, processing, and refining.
